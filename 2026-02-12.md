## RTK accuracy test:

The module was connected to Ntrip server and left enter fixed mode.

Then the current position data were recorded and the antenna position was shifted to a new position.

The New positions points were recorded and and processed with Haversine's formula (c code).

And the result where tested with physical measurements. Accuracy was <10cm for multiple readings. And bearing angle out put is in the range of -180 to 180.

North - $0\degree$
East -  $90\degree$ 
South - $180\degree$
West - $270\degree$

Above is the standard angles for the directions, all the calculated output angles lie in the respective angle range.

Distance output is in the same unit of the radius of earth, and thus both distance and bearing must be mapped before using in actual firmware.

Fixed mode stayed throughout the testing unchanged as antenna was in complete open area for the entire test duration.
