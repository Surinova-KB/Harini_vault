
 1. How to navigate from start to destination - long term decision
 2. Spontaneous decisions that needs to be made in order to reach the destination - short term decision

   Predictive Planning :

   - Make predictions about other vehicles and how they are moving, Then use these predictions to inform our decisions.
   - Predicts information based on other agents in the environment.

Reactive Planning :

  One method to address the challenge of multilevel decision-making is **reactive planning**. In reactive planning, we define sets of rules that take into account the current state of the ego vehicle and other objects in the environment and produce immediate actions.

  Considers only the current states not future predictions.
